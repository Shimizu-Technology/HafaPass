### What Every Scan Screen Means

| Color / Screen | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Green — Admitted** *(clears in ~1 s)* | Welcome **1 person** in. Stamp wrist/hand now *(Option B only)*. |
| **Blue — Exited** *(Option A only)* | Guest checked out; they can scan back in later. |
| **Amber — Check ticket / Already inside / Wait to re-enter** *(stays until tapped)* | **Hold guest.** Tap to dismiss, read the reason below the camera, send to **Door lead**. |
| **Red — Do not admit / Invalid ticket / Re-entry limit reached** *(stays until tapped)* | **Do not admit.** Tap to dismiss, try **Find an attendee**, or send to **Door lead**. |
| **No working QR** | Scroll to **Find an attendee**, search name or `HP-T...`, confirm match, tap **Admit**. |

## BACK (Page 2) — Ticket List Refresh, Offline Mode, Backup List & Close

### Refreshing the Saved Ticket List

- **When:** 15–30 min before doors · at division breaks · after a burst of walk-up sales/refunds · right after reconnecting.
- **How:** Confirm **Online** → tap **Sync now**.
- **Is it current?** Check **Ticket list ready · N tickets**, future Guam expiry time, and **Waiting to sync: 0** *(exact version is inside **Device details**)*.

### If Wi-Fi or Cell Signal Drops (Offline Mode)

1. **Keep scanning** while the saved ticket list is valid. Scans stay saved on your phone (**Waiting to sync** rises).
2. **NEVER** sign out, switch accounts, clear browser data, or tap **Reset trusted device** while **Waiting to sync > 0**.
3. Send confusing duplicates to the **Door lead** (other offline phones cannot see your queued scans yet).
4. When **Online** returns, tap **Sync now** and wait for **Waiting to sync: 0**.
5. If your saved ticket list expires offline, pause the lane, leave the browser tab open, and switch to the Manager Desk backup list.

### Backup Attendee List (Only When Door Lead Calls It)

*Data safety: exported on the organizer's laptop only (never a personal phone); no photos, screenshots, or group-chat sharing; delete CSV from every device after the event.*

1. Look up guest by **Name** or **Order #** (`HP-...`) on the Manager Desk backup list.
2. Check **Status:** **`Valid`** *(or **`Paid`** in Orders view)* = admit. **`Checked in`**, **`Cancelled`**, **`Refunded`**, **`Partially refunded`**, or **`Transferred`** = **send to Door lead**.
3. Cross off the row with **time, gate, and your initials**. Log any unexported receipt on the **numbered paper exception log** with Door lead approval.
4. Once online, the Door lead checks off manually admitted tickets via **Find an attendee** so final counts match. Return all printed lists to the Door lead.

### End-of-Night Checklist

- [ ] Confirm **Online**, tap **Sync now**, wait until **Waiting to sync: 0**.
- [ ] Door lead checks **Flagged offline conflicts** and **Recent admissions**.
- [ ] Return printed lists and exception log to Door lead (organizer deletes backup CSVs).
- [ ] Wait for Door lead all-clear before signing out; plug phone and battery pack in to charge.
