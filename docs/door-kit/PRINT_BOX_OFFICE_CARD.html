Cash Sale

  1. Open Dashboard → Copa de Marianas → Box office.
  2. Tap + / − for Adult, Child, and Under 5 tickets. (Optional: type buyer name and email.)
  3. Collect and count the cash first. Never tap Process sale before cash is in your hand.
  4. Select Cash, then tap Process sale · $XX.XX.
  5. On the green Sale complete! card, deliver the tickets:
    • Show QR: Full-screen QR per ticket (Ticket 1 of N) for the buyer to photograph.
    • Print tickets: Prints one QR ticket per page from the browser.
    • Email tickets: Enter or confirm buyer email, then tap Send tickets.
  6. Reloaded by mistake? Refreshing the same browser tab restores the last completed cash sale so you can still show, print, or email those tickets.
  7. Signal dropped mid-sale? If you see "A saved sale needs confirmation", tap Retry saved sale — never ring up a second sale.

Pre-Doors Check (finish 15–30 min before doors open)

  • Count starting cash float with Door lead; small bills ready for change.
  • Signed into your own HåfaPass account with Box office open on the Copa event.
  • Confirmed whether pay-by-card options are turned on; printer paper and battery tested (if printing).

Pay by Card (only when enabled for Copa)

  1. Select tickets and show the buyer the all-in total under Payment method (includes the online service fee).
  2. Choose one option:
    • Pay on buyer's phone (default): Tap Prepare Stripe checkout → buyer scans Scan to pay QR (or tap Email payment link if email entered).
    • Enter card on this device (Door lead only / last resort when buyer cannot pay on their own phone — never write down a card number or repeat it aloud): Tap Prepare Stripe checkout → Open Stripe checkout → enter card on Stripe's payment page → return to HåfaPass.
  3. Tickets hold for 35 minutes. Tap Cancel sale right away if the buyer walks away before paying.
  4. Wait for Paid — tickets ready, show the ticket QR(s) (or tap Email tickets), then tap Start next sale.

Card Recovery Rules

SituationWhat to do
Tab closed / buyer stepped aside Tap Open card checkouts at the top of Box office → reopen that order (Show payment QR, Open checkout, or Show tickets). Never start a duplicate sale.
Paid — waiting for confirmation Tell the buyer: "Your payment went through; your tickets will appear in a moment." STOP — do NOT charge again and do NOT enter Cash. Tap Check payment status until Paid — tickets ready appears. Call Door lead / on-call if stuck ~2 min (confirm wait time with on-call).
Failed (declined) Buyer can retry on the same checkout, or tap Cancel sale.
Expired Tap Check payment status first to confirm Stripe did not capture late before taking another payment.
