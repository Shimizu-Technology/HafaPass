Pre-Doors Check (finish 15–30 min before doors open)

  1. Phone 100% charged + battery pack plugged in; screen bright; volume up; set Auto-Lock / Screen timeout to Never.
  2. Sign into HåfaPass in Safari or Chrome with your own staff login. Open Scanner (screen title says Check-in) and choose Copa de Marianas.

  4. Tap Sync now once while online (15–30 minutes before doors open).
  5. Tap Start camera, allow camera permission, leave Sound on, and scan 1 test QR.

Re-entry or Hand-Stamp Rule — Choose One Before Event Day (Do Not Mix)

☐ Option A — Exit scan in HåfaPass
Keep green ENTRY selected on incoming lanes. Use blue EXIT only at the exit lane. No hand stamps. If you see Already inside, the guest must scan out at Exit first or see the Door lead.
☐ Option B — Hand stamp + Re-entry Off
Scan QR once on first arrival, then stamp hand/wrist. Returning guests show their stamp at the marked re-entry lane (do not re-scan QR — it stays checked in).
Override rule: Only the Door lead (signed in with a lead role) can override. Volunteers never override. Never override on a guest's word alone — the reason must record what the Door lead actually checked.

Refreshing the Saved Ticket List

  • When: 15–30 min before doors · at division breaks · after a burst of walk-up sales/refunds · right after coming back online.
  • How: Confirm Online, then tap Sync now.
  • How to tell it is current: Check Ticket list ready · N tickets, a future Guam expiry time, and Waiting to sync: 0 (exact version is inside Device details).

If Wi-Fi or Cell Signal Drops (Offline Mode)

  1. Keep scanning as long as the saved ticket list has not expired. Each scan stays saved on your phone and raises Waiting to sync.
  2. NEVER sign out, switch accounts, clear browser history/site data, or tap Reset trusted device while Waiting to sync > 0.
  3. Send confusing duplicates to the Door lead — other offline phones cannot see your queued scans yet.
  4. As soon as Online returns, tap Sync now and wait for Waiting to sync to reach 0.
  5. If your saved ticket list expires while offline, pause that lane, leave the browser tab open so saved scans stay safe, and switch to the Manager Desk backup list.

Backup Attendee List (Only When Door Lead Calls It)

Attendee data safety: Export on the organizer's laptop only (never a personal phone). No photos, no screenshots, and no sharing in group chats. Delete the CSV from every device after the event.
  1. Search the backup list at the Manager Desk by Name or Order # (HP-…).
  2. Check Status: Valid (or Paid in Orders view) = OK to admit. Checked in, Cancelled, Refunded, Partially refunded, or Transferred = Do not admit; send to the Door lead.
  3. Cross off or highlight the row with time, gate, and your initials. Log any unexported receipt on the numbered paper exception log with Door lead approval.
  4. Once scanners are back online, the Door lead checks off every manually admitted ticket via Find an attendee so final counts match.
  5. Return every printed list and exception log to the Door lead at close.

End-of-Night Checklist

  • Confirm Online, tap Sync now, and wait until Waiting to sync: 0.
  • Door lead reviews Flagged offline conflicts and Recent admissions on Check-in.
  • Return printed lists and numbered exception log to Door lead (organizer deletes backup CSV files from every device).
  • Wait for Door lead all-clear before signing out; plug phone and battery pack in to charge.
