## Facts to test with professionals

- Organizers are intended ticket sellers; Shimizu Technology LLC, operating HåfaPass, is their limited collection agent. Buyers pay the Shimizu Stripe account and organizer funds reach its business account. Checks are written after events, net of fees, refunds, chargebacks and adjustments.
- The intended fee is 5% plus $1.00 per paid ticket, with card processing absorbed by HåfaPass. The setting is configurable and needs live verification. All-in buyer pricing is the intended presentation.
- Paid payouts require organizer tax information. Admin reports prepare monthly service-fee and annual organizer data. They do not file BPT or 1099 returns, match TINs, or implement backup withholding.
- The Compiler's current [11 GCA §26202](https://col.guamcourts.gov/sites/default/files/11gc026_Q.pdf) states a 4% general BPT rate beginning October 1, 2026; §26203.2 has conditional small-business relief. The CPA must decide the applicable base, class and rate. Fee-only BPT is a working position, not a verified DRT ruling.
- [11 GCA Ch. 22 Art. 3](https://col.guamcourts.gov/sites/default/files/11gc022.pdf) labels the former admission tax repealed. Recreation-facility and activity-specific levies remain for review.
- [FinCEN FIN-2014-R009](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/administrative_ruling/FIN-2014-R009.pdf) makes check disbursement a material risk for the federal payment-processor exclusion. The organizer agency clause alone does not resolve it. [11 GCA §109102](https://col.guamcourts.gov/sites/default/files/11gc109.pdf) also reaches some domestic transmissions by instrument or order; counsel must decide its application.
- Stripe's [restricted-business list](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses) includes payment facilitation/aggregation. Stripe's [territory guidance](https://support.stripe.com/questions/stripe-availability-for-outlying-territories-of-supported-countries) and the Nevada account details require provider/legal review.
