# Copa de Marianas 2027 volunteer door guide

**Saturday, January 30 · UOG Calvo Field House**
Door lead ____________________  Backup ____________________  HåfaPass on call ____________________

Print a copy per lane. The organizer supplies the door and cash-table team. HåfaPass trains the team and provides the scanner, setup guide and on-call support.

## Before opening

- Charge your phone and bring a cable or battery. Open your staff invitation and sign in on your own phone in Safari or Chrome. Open **Scanner**, select the event and let the organizer register your browser if prompted.
- Give your phone a lane name. While online, download the list and tap **Sync now**. Check that the list is current, the device is authorized and the queue says zero. Start the camera, allow permission and scan the practice ticket. Try entering a QR code manually.
- Agree on the stamp, the marked re-entry lane, the manager desk and the exception log. Keep the timestamped emergency attendee CSV or door list at the manager desk. Treat it as private.

## At the door

| If you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Admitted** (green) | Let one person in. Stamp after their first admission if the organizer approved stamp re-entry. |
| **Check ticket** / duplicate (amber) | Send the guest to the door lead. Check whether the ticket was already admitted or queued on another device. |
| **Do not admit** (red) | Read the reason. Try the printed QR code or the attendee lookup with the door lead. Escalate cancelled, transferred or unpaid tickets. |
| Guest returns with a stamp | At the re-entry lane, the lead checks the approved event stamp and admits the returning guest under the agreed policy. Record re-entry in the lane tally. The original QR remains checked in. |
| Guest has no working QR | Search by attendee details in **Scanner**. The lead confirms the match and uses normal check-in. When electronic lookup is unavailable, the lead may use the printed list and numbered exception log. |

## If the network drops

- Keep the scanner open. With a valid downloaded list, scan locally and watch the queued count. An offline admit is provisional. Route confusing duplicates to one lead since other offline phones cannot see your queue.
- On reconnect, tap **Sync now**, wait for the queue to reach zero, and have the lead review conflicts. If the list expires, authorization fails or the queue will not drain, pause that lane and preserve the browser and its saved scans. Use the manager desk for the approved paper fallback.
- Escalate with the lane, time, screen message and ticket/order reference through the private team channel. Keep buyer details off public chats.

## At close

Sync to zero, hand over the exception log and return printed copies to the lead. The lead reconciles paper admissions, duplicate conflicts and the final door counts. Only then retire devices and dispose of the printed attendee data using the organizer's retention policy.

Rehearse with three phones before the December 28 check. Prove camera permission, valid and duplicate QR, out-of-date manifest, manual lookup, the paper log, stamp re-entry and offline reconciliation. See the [full operations runbook](EVENT_DAY_OPERATIONS.md).
