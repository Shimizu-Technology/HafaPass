# Set up your event on HåfaPass

**You run the doors.** Your organization provides the scanners, door lead and cash-table staff. HåfaPass gives you the software, a setup session, training for your team, a printable door guide and someone on call during the event. Before selling tickets, agree on the event lead, a backup and the HåfaPass support contact.

## Set up ticket sales

1. Create the organizer account under the organization's legal name. Request verification from the dashboard. For paid sales, complete tax information and set up the approved payout destination in **Settings → Payouts**. Share financial details only through the approved secure process.
2. Create a general admission event. Enter the venue, Guam date and time, doors-open time, sales window and a clear description of who needs a ticket. For Copa, this is spectator admission. Send athletes to ASJJF for competition registration.
3. Create one ticket type per age or price band. Add a $0 **Under 5** option if the organizer adopts that rule. Issue those tickets against capacity. Confirm where the age boundary falls before publishing. Copa's 2026 references list $12 child and $17 adult and contain overlapping wording for age four. Confirm the 2027 bands with the organizer.
4. Agree on spectator capacity with the venue after accounting for competitors, staff and all reserved space. Set the event cap and ticket quantities. Budget room for paid tickets, free tickets, guest-list comps and retail/door cash. Create actual comp tickets through the guest list so each attendee has a QR.
5. For Copa, use buyer-paid HåfaPass fees of 5% + $1 per paid ticket, included in the price shown to the buyer. Shimizu covers card processing. Review the displayed all-in prices before publishing. Put the approved refund policy and re-entry rule on the event page. Preview on a phone and test Adult, Child and Under 5 together before sharing the URL.

## Retail cash sales and check-in

1. Set the cash outlet, inventory allocation, sale hours and till owner with the event lead. For an approved retail partner such as Deal Depot, assign named staff the minimum Box Office permissions for this event. Each person uses their own account.
2. In **Box Office**, select the exact event and ticket types. Receive and count the cash before recording the sale. Give buyers the issued QR tickets. Keep a shift cash record and reconcile the drawer to the Box Office summary. Have the organizer confirm how and when retail cash is handed over.
3. Train staff with the [Volunteer door guide](VOLUNTEER_DOOR_GUIDE.md) and [operations drill](EVENT_DAY_OPERATIONS.md#three-device-offline-drill). Before opening, have every device download the list, scan a practice QR, test manual lookup and reconnect with its queue empty.
4. After closing, sync and reconcile all scanners, cash drawers and incidents. Reconcile refunds and disputes before finalizing settlements.

## Copa payout schedule

Shimizu collects card sales through its Stripe account. Under the November 24 decision, Shimizu sends the available amount by bank transfer **2–3 business days after the event**, with about **10%** of the payable proceeds reserved for chargebacks. Release the remaining reserve after **30 days**, after reconciling deductions. Physical cash kept by the organizer is accounted for in the statement. Finance records each confirmed transfer and keeps the bank receipt privately. The organizer can review settlement versions and payout records in **Finances**. Bank-transfer setup and this sequence need acceptance before the launch.
