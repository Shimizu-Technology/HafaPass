# How to Read the Monthly CPA Custodial & BPT CSV Export

**Audience:** CPA and platform finance admin.
**Status:** Preparation aid only — not a filed Guam BPT (Form GRT-1) or income tax return. Fee-only BPT treatment and organizer-vs-platform tax allocations are working positions for CPA and Guam counsel confirmation, not settled tax rulings.

## Where to download it

1. Open **Admin → Tax reports** (`/admin/tax-reports`).
2. Select the **Calendar year** and **Guam calendar month** (`Pacific/Guam`, ChST, UTC+10 year-round, no daylight saving time).
3. Review the on-screen summary separating **Custodial liability (organizer proceeds)** from **HåfaPass fee revenue & Guam BPT estimate**, then click **Export monthly CPA CSV**.
4. The file downloads as `hafapass-cpa-monthly-YYYY-MM.csv` (`Cache-Control: no-store`). Every download is audit-logged (`tax_report.exported` with `export: "monthly_cpa_custodial_bpt"`).

## Recording-time period-stability rule, scope, and privacy

- **Recording-time immutability (monetary & roll-forward columns stay byte-identical):** A closed Guam calendar month is defined strictly by **recording time (`created_at`)** — the month each immutable ledger row or append-only audit row was created — **never** by a back-dated or future confirmation/effective date supplied later, **never** by a current mutable status (such as `stripe_fee_evidences.status` or `disputes.status`), and **never** by a mutable column like `updated_at`. When no outer database transaction is already open (the normal web/export path), all report aggregates execute inside a single PostgreSQL `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` transaction snapshot so refund and allocation totals cannot drift mid-export; if invoked inside an already-open outer transaction, it inherits the caller's isolation level.
  - *Qualification on W-9 columns:* All **monetary, roll-forward, reserve, exception, bridge, and BPT columns** in a closed month stay byte-identical across later activity and audit retention redaction. The informational `w9_nonprofit_classification` and `nonprofit_exemption_note` columns reflect each organization's **current W-9 at export time**, so updating an organization's W-9 later changes only those informational columns (never any numeric balance).
  - **Custodial Stripe captures & late backfills (`proceeds_collected`, `fee_revenue_gross`):** Only live/legacy non-simulated USD Stripe captures with `payments.created_at < month_end` establish custody for a month, and order custodial booking time is `GREATEST(COALESCE(orders.completed_at, orders.created_at), first_capture_created_at)`. If a legacy order completed in October gets a backfilled Stripe capture in November (`StripeWebhookProcessor#backfill_legacy_payment`), October remains unchanged (order stays in October's bridge) and November books the custodial reclassification and platform fee with `prior_period_note` `"Stripe capture backfill relates to Oct 2026"`.
  - **Never-fulfilled captures (`buyer_refund_captures`, `buyer_refunds_paid`, `fee_revenue_gross`, `fee_refunds`):** A card payment can succeed after HåfaPass has already released its order: the hold expired, the inventory was released, or every pass night was cancelled first (common with door-card payment links). The order is never fulfilled, no tickets are issued, and the whole payment is refunded automatically. HåfaPass identifies these orders with one shared test, `Commerce::NeverFulfilledCapture`, which `Settlements::Calculator` and the payout statement also use: a null `orders.completed_at`, plus a live USD Stripe capture on the order whose own late-capture reconciliation exception (codes `late_payment_success_after_hold_expiry`, `late_payment_success_after_inventory_release`, `pass_all_included_nights_cancelled`, linked to that payment) was recorded within one hour of the capture. OrderLifecycle writes both in one transaction. The test depends only on those two rows, not on the month being reported, so a capture at 23:59:59 whose exception is stamped 00:00:00 the next day is booked as owed to the buyer in both months and the roll-forward holds. An exception added later than the window never reclassifies a capture already booked. A legacy completed order with a null `completed_at` but no such exception stays in organizer custody. Each movement is booked in its own recording month:
    - **Capture month:** booked at the capture's recording time, `GREATEST(COALESCE(payments.succeeded_at, payments.created_at), payments.created_at)`, never the earlier reservation month (which stays byte-identical), with `prior_period_note` `"Late capture relates to <Mon YYYY>"` when the order was created in an earlier month. The ticket-price share (`order_items.organizer_proceeds_cents`) goes to `buyer_refund_captures`, **not** `proceeds_collected`, because it is owed back to the buyer and never reaches an organizer payout. The HåfaPass service fee goes to `fee_revenue_gross`, as in the BPT detail CSV.
    - **Refund month:** the refund's success month. The ticket-price share goes to `buyer_refunds_paid`, **not** `proceeds_refunded`. The service fee goes to `fee_refunds`.
    - **Lost dispute:** a lost dispute returns the buyer's money through the card network, so it is **not** a `chargeback` against organizer custody. Its ticket-price share (the dispute amount allocated pro rata between the ticket-price share and the rest of the payment) goes to `buyer_refunds_paid` in the month the dispute was lost (`COALESCE(dispute_lost_audit.created_at, disputes.closed_at)`). The service-fee share stays in fee revenue (confirm the BPT treatment with the CPA).
    - **Returned twice:** clearing is capped per order at the ticket-price share captured, so a refund after a lost dispute, or a lost dispute after a refund, never clears the buyer balance twice or drives it negative. The excess is a HåfaPass loss outside organizer custody: the row sets `reconciliation_exception_flag = yes` and `reconciliation_exception_note` says `Never-fulfilled payment returned to the buyer twice (refund and lost dispute)` with the cumulative and in-month amounts.
    - **Between the two:** if the capture and refund fall either side of a month-end, the earlier month closes with the ticket-price share in `closing_buyer_refunds_owed` and the service fee in that month's fee revenue. The next month opens with `opening_buyer_refunds_owed`, and its `fee_refunds` reverse the fee. Organizer payables stay at `$0.00` throughout, matching the organizer's payout statement.
    - **Stripe processing** on these orders is never deducted from an organizer. It appears only in `stripe_processing_costs_informational`, and `Settlements::Calculator` books it as processing covered by HåfaPass, so the payout statement agrees. **Needs Leon/CPA confirmation:** this bearer rule is a working position, kept in one switch (`Commerce::NeverFulfilledCapture::ORGANIZER_BEARS_PROCESSING`) that both readers follow.
  - **Paid payouts (`organizer_payouts_made`):** Recorded in the Guam month the payout's append-only paid audit (`payout.reconciled` → `paid` or `payout.paid`) was created (`COALESCE(paid_audit.created_at, payouts.paid_at)`). Audit joins rely on immutable fields that survive `DataRetention.anonymize!` (`action`, `auditable_id`, `created_at`), not solely `after_data`, so retention redaction leaves closed months unchanged. Covers **every** live (non-simulated) USD payout on every recognized event/organization, independent of whether a Stripe order exists.
  - **Payout reversals (`payout_reversals`):** Recorded strictly in the Guam month the surviving-field reversal audit row (`payout.reconciled` → `reversed` or `payout.reversed`) was created (`rev_audit.created_at`). A redacted `payout.reconciled` row counts as a reversal only when an earlier paid/reconciled audit exists on the same payout (`earlier_a.id < a.id`), preventing a lone redacted paid audit from being misread as a reversal. User-supplied bank `confirmed_at` dates never rewrite an earlier closed month, and the report **never** falls back to mutable `payouts.updated_at`. If a reversed payout has no reversal audit row, it is **not** injected into closed-month CSV rows (so an auditless November corruption cannot alter October's CSV); instead it is surfaced separately in the JSON preview's top-level `current_integrity_alerts` block (`as_of: "current_at_export_time"`, excluded from closed-month CSV).
  - **Actual Stripe processing fees (`organizer_processing_deducted`, `stripe_processing_costs_informational`):** Dated strictly by the immutable append-only `FeeComponent` recording timestamp (`fee_components.created_at`), completely independent of current mutable `stripe_fee_evidences.status`.
  - **Balance adjustments & reserves (`other_adjustments`, `reserve_held`, `reserve_released`):** Dated strictly by `balance_adjustments.created_at` (never overridden by past or future `effective_at`, which remains for `Settlements::Calculator` payout eligibility), and scoped by immutable payment custody (`order_id` in live Stripe orders or `NULL`; `dispute_id` in live Stripe disputes or `NULL`, completely independent of whether the dispute is currently `open`, `won`, or `lost`).
  - **Lost disputes (`chargebacks`):** Dated by `COALESCE(dispute_lost_audit.created_at, disputes.closed_at)` — never mutable `disputes.updated_at`. Auditless lost disputes that also lack `closed_at` have no reliable loss timestamp (`disputes.created_at` records when the dispute opened, not when it was lost); they are excluded from closed-month chargebacks and flagged in top-level `current_integrity_alerts`.
  - **Prior-period relation labels (`prior_period_note`):** When a movement recorded in Month M relates to an earlier closed month — including a late Stripe capture backfill (`"Stripe capture backfill relates to Oct 2026"`), a payout or reversal (`"Payout relates to Oct 2026"` / `"Payout reversal relates to Oct 2026"`), a late processing fee (`"Processing fee relates to Oct 2026"`), a chargeback lost for an earlier dispute (`"Chargeback relates to Oct 2026"`), or a back-dated custodial or bridge adjustment (`"Adjustment relates to Oct 2026"` / `"Bridge adjustment relates to Oct 2026"`) — Month M includes the movement and labels it in `prior_period_note` while October's monetary/roll-forward columns stay byte-identical.
- **Live USD Stripe agent captures + all recognized custodial/settlement activity:** Custodial columns include completed/partially-refunded/refunded orders backed by live (or legacy non-test, non-simulated) USD Stripe captures created before `month_end`, all in-scope USD `BalanceAdjustment`s (including adjustment-only events and organization-level `event_id: nil` adjustments), and all live USD payouts (including payouts on adjustment-only or non-Stripe events). Test, simulated, organizer-retained door cash, organizer-direct Clover sales, and non-USD orders are excluded from custodial liability. Non-custodial non-cash sales (and any settlement adjustments or disputes linked to non-custodial orders/payments) are bridged in `non_custodial_settlement_bridge` across **all** events, including Clover-only events.
- **No PII:** Rows identify organizations and events strictly by numeric `organization_id` and `event_id`. No organizer names, event titles, buyer names, emails, addresses, or TINs appear in the file.
- **Spreadsheet-safe:** Formula-like text values are neutralized; signed numeric amounts remain plain decimal numbers (`-1.10`) so spreadsheet sums work directly.
- **Included rows:** Candidate events are built as the union of **every** activity source (custodial sales/refunds/chargebacks/processing, adjustments, payouts, reversals, and non-custodial settlement bridge). Every month ends with one `row_type = total` row (all `$0.00` on a zero-activity month).

### 2. Custodial Liability Roll-Forward (Balance Sheet — Organizer Funds Held in Custody)

Under our working position (pending CPA/counsel confirmation), these funds belong to the event organizers, **not** Shimizu Technology LLC revenue.

| Column | Timing (Guam time) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `opening_custodial_liability` | Before 1st of month | Organizer proceeds held by HåfaPass at the start of the month (equals prior month's `closing_custodial_liability`). Includes adjustment-only events/orgs. |
| `proceeds_collected` | `GREATEST(order completed_at, first_capture_created_at)` | Organizer proceeds from tickets (and any add-ons/donations), net of discounts and organizer-absorbed service fees. Excludes never-fulfilled captures (see `buyer_refund_captures`). |
| `proceeds_refunded` | Refund success month | Allocated organizer proceeds returned to buyers on succeeded refunds (`RefundItem.organizer_proceeds_cents`; subtracted from liability). Excludes refunds of never-fulfilled captures (see `buyer_refunds_paid`). |
| `chargebacks` | `COALESCE(dispute_lost_audit.created_at, disputes.closed_at)` | Lost Stripe disputes (`disputes.payment_id` → live Stripe capture) debited against organizer proceeds (subtracted from liability). Excludes lost disputes on never-fulfilled captures (see `buyer_refunds_paid`). |
| `organizer_processing_deducted` | `FeeComponent.created_at` month | Actual Stripe card processing deducted from organizer proceeds, dated strictly by immutable `fee_components.created_at` (independent of current `stripe_fee_evidences.status`). Uses the exact `Settlements::Calculator` split. |
| `other_adjustments` | `BalanceAdjustment.created_at` month | Net posted/reversed non-reserve balance adjustments scoped by immutable payment custody (independent of dispute status; never overridden by past or future `effective_at`; signed; added to liability). |
| `organizer_payouts_made` | `COALESCE(paid_audit.created_at, payouts.paid_at)` | Gross live USD payouts (`amount_cents`) recorded as paid in the month across all recognized events (including adjustment-only events; subtracted from liability). Survives `DataRetention.anonymize!`. |
| `payout_reversals` | `rev_audit.created_at` | Gross amount of previously paid payouts reversed in the month, dated strictly by the surviving-field reversal audit row's `created_at` (added back to custodial liability). Keeps the original paid month unchanged. |
| `closing_custodial_liability` | End of month | Signed net custodial roll-forward balance at month-end (includes held reserves). |
| `closing_custodial_payables` | End of month (Balance Sheet Liability) | **Total owed BY HåfaPass to organizers.** On `total`, summed **per organization** (`SUM over orgs of MAX(org_net_closing, 0)`): positive and negative event balances offset **only within the same `organization_id`**, never netting unrelated organizers against one another. |
| `closing_organizer_deficits` | End of month (Balance Sheet Receivable) | **Total owed TO HåfaPass by organizers** (negative balances from post-payout refunds/chargebacks). On `total`, summed **per organization** (`SUM over orgs of MAX(-org_net_closing, 0)`). Exact check: `closing_custodial_payables - closing_organizer_deficits = closing_custodial_liability`. |
| `opening_buyer_refunds_owed` | Before 1st of month | Ticket-price share of never-fulfilled captures still owed back to buyers at the start of the month (equals the prior month's `closing_buyer_refunds_owed`). **Not** organizer money. |
| `buyer_refund_captures` | Capture recording time, `GREATEST(COALESCE(payments.succeeded_at, payments.created_at), payments.created_at)` | Ticket-price share (`order_items.organizer_proceeds_cents`) of live Stripe captures on never-fulfilled orders recorded in the month. |
| `buyer_refunds_paid` | Refund success month, or dispute loss month | Ticket-price share returned to buyers on never-fulfilled captures: succeeded refunds (`RefundItem.organizer_proceeds_cents`) and lost disputes (pro rata ticket-price share of the dispute amount), capped per order at the ticket-price share captured. |
| `closing_buyer_refunds_owed` | End of month (Balance Sheet Liability) | **Refunds owed to buyers** for payments HåfaPass received but never fulfilled, usually an automatic refund that succeeds early the next month. A separate liability from organizer custody: `closing_buyer_refunds_owed = opening_buyer_refunds_owed + buyer_refund_captures - buyer_refunds_paid`. The service-fee share of the same payments is in fee revenue until `fee_refunds` reverses it, so the full amount owed back is `closing_buyer_refunds_owed` plus those unrefunded fees. |

**Horizontal roll-forward identity (exact on every row and on `total`):**

```text
closing_custodial_liability
  = opening_custodial_liability
  + proceeds_collected
  - proceeds_refunded
  - chargebacks
  - organizer_processing_deducted
  + other_adjustments
  - organizer_payouts_made
  + payout_reversals
```

### 3. Reserve, Reconciliation Exceptions, Prior-Period Notes, Settlement Bridge & Withholding Memos

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `reserve_held` | New `reserve_hold` balance adjustments recorded (`created_at`) in the month (e.g., ~10% post-event holdback). Shown as a positive amount. |
| `reserve_released` | `reserve_release` balance adjustments recorded (`created_at`) in the month (e.g., 30-day release). Shown as a positive amount. |
| `ending_reserve_balance` | Cumulative net reserve still held at month-end. **Note:** Held reserves are *part of* `closing_custodial_liability`, restricting how much is currently payable. |
| `unallocated_refunds_exception` | In-month succeeded Stripe refunds whose `refunds.amount_cents` exceeds `SUM(refund_items.amount_cents)` (e.g., legacy refunds created without `RefundItem` allocations). |
| `cumulative_unallocated_refunds_exception` | Cumulative unallocated legacy refunds through month-end (the cumulative balance that triggers `reconciliation_exception_flag` even in later months where in-month unallocated refunds are `$0.00`). |
| `reconciliation_exception_flag` | `yes` if the row has any in-month or cumulative unallocated legacy refunds, or a never-fulfilled payment returned to the buyer twice; `no` otherwise. *(Current-state auditless reversed payouts and auditless `closed_at`-null lost disputes are reported outside the closed-month CSV in top-level `current_integrity_alerts` so closed-month CSVs never mutate retroactively.)* |
| `reconciliation_exception_note` | Plain-language summary of unallocated legacy refund exceptions (`$X.XX cumulative ($Y.YY in month)`) and of never-fulfilled payments returned to the buyer twice. |
| `prior_period_note` | Labels any movement recorded in this month that relates to an earlier closed Guam month (late Stripe capture backfill, payout, payout reversal, late processing fee, chargeback for earlier dispute, back-dated custodial adjustment, or back-dated bridge adjustment). Blank when all in-month movements belong to the current month. |
| `non_custodial_settlement_bridge` | Cumulative net settlement-only activity on the event that appears in `Settlements::Calculator` but is **not** held in HåfaPass Stripe custody: organizer-direct `boh_clover` card-present sales minus their refunds/processing/disputes, non-agent disputes, and event-level settlement adjustments linked to non-custodial orders or non-agent disputes. Populated even on Clover-only events with zero Stripe sales. Always `$0.00` for pure online Stripe events. |
| `backup_withholding_memo` | Net backup withholding (`withheld_cents + carried_withholding_cents`) from payouts recorded as paid in the month **minus** payouts reversed in the month. Track separately as a tax-withholding remittance liability until deposited. |

**Exact reconciliation to organizer payout statements (`Settlements::Calculator`):**

For any event (pure Stripe, adjustment-only, Clover-only, or mixed-tender Stripe + Clover + door cash + linked adjustments) whose activity was recorded on or before month-end (with `effective_at <= month_end`, zero unallocated refund exceptions, and zero `current_integrity_alerts`):
1. Compute `Settlements::Calculator`'s true signed unpaid balance **without double-counting shortfalls**:
   - If `negative_balance_cents > 0`: `unpaid_balance = -negative_balance_cents`
   - Otherwise: `unpaid_balance = payable_cents - paid_cents`
   *(Equivalently: `organizer_proceeds_cents - processing_fee_cents - reserve_cents + adjustment_cents - paid_cents`.)*
2. Then:
   ```text
   closing_custodial_liability - ending_reserve_balance + non_custodial_settlement_bridge
     == Settlements::Calculator unpaid_balance
   ```
3. And `ending_reserve_balance == Settlements::Calculator reserve_cents`.
4. `closing_buyer_refunds_owed` is **not** part of this identity. Never-fulfilled orders never reach an organizer payout statement, so their balance sits only in the buyer-refund columns. *(Note: an adjustment created in Month M with a future `effective_at` in Month M+1 is booked in CPA Month M by `created_at` while `Settlements::Calculator` applies it starting at `effective_at`.)*

Never-fulfilled captures: capture recording time, `GREATEST(COALESCE(payments.succeeded_at, payments.created_at), payments.created_at)` | Gross HåfaPass ticketing service fees (both buyer-paid and organizer-absorbed) on custodial Stripe captures. A never-fulfilled capture's fee is booked in its capture month and reversed by `fee_refunds` in its refund month, matching the BPT detail CSV. |
| `fee_refunds` | Refund success month | Allocated HåfaPass service fees returned on succeeded refunds (`fee_cents + organizer_fee_cents`). |

| `stripe_processing_costs_informational` | `FeeComponent.created_at` month | Total actual (non-estimated) Stripe processing costs recorded in the month (`fee_components.created_at`), independent of current `stripe_fee_evidences.status`. **Informational expense only** — does not reduce Guam BPT gross receipts. |
| `bpt_base_fee_revenue_estimate` | Monthly net | Working BPT base equal to `fee_revenue_net` under the fee-only working position (confirm with CPA). |

Periods from **April 1, 2018 – September 30, 2025** show the historical **`5.00`%** rate (P.L. 34-87). Periods **before April 2018** emit **`not_supported`** (and blank estimated BPT) — confirm historical rates with the CPA. Estimate only; does not apply conditional § 26203.2 3% small-business relief. |
| `estimated_bpt_on_fee_revenue_estimate` | **Estimate only** | Estimated Guam BPT on `MAX(bpt_base_fee_revenue_estimate, 0)` at `estimated_bpt_rate_pct` (half-up cent rounding; `$0.00` when an event's net fee base is negative; blank for pre-April 2018 `not_supported` periods). |
| `currency` | Always `USD` | Reporting currency. |

## Important CPA reminders & deferred Clover residuals

- **Why event-level BPT estimates do not always sum to the `total` row BPT estimate:** Each `event` row floors `estimated_bpt_on_fee_revenue_estimate` at `$0.00` when `fee_revenue_net` is negative (for example, an event with cross-month refunds and no new sales). On the `total` row, Guam Form GRT-1 is computed at the **entity level** on `MAX(SUM(bpt_base_fee_revenue_estimate), 0)`, where negative net-fee events offset positive net-fee events in the same filing month (plus up to ±$0.01/row half-up rounding differences).
- **W-9 nonprofit classification (current at export time) vs. Guam DRT exemption & HåfaPass fees:** `w9_nonprofit_classification = yes` reflects the organizer's **current** W-9 federal tax classification at export time (not a historical snapshot as of the reporting month, so a later W-9 update can change these informational columns); `drt_exemption_verified` is always `not_tracked` until verified DRT tracking is added. Confirm DRT exemption certificates externally. Under our working position (confirm with CPA), HåfaPass's own service fees on nonprofit events remain in Shimizu Technology LLC's BPT base.
- **Current integrity alerts (outside closed-month CSV):** Auditless reversed payouts (no reversal audit row) and auditless lost disputes without `closed_at` (where `disputes.created_at` cannot establish loss timing) are intentionally kept out of closed-month CSV rows and surfaced in the JSON preview's `current_integrity_alerts` block so corrupt/auditless status mutations never rewrite closed months.
- **Known limitation: payouts with no paid audit after retention redaction.** If a payout has no `payout.paid` / paid `payout.reconciled` audit at all and its only reconciliation audit is later redacted by data retention, the report cannot tell whether that redacted row was the payment or the reversal. `current_integrity_alerts` flags missing **reversal** audits, not missing paid audits. Until the data-retention follow-up keeps each audit's transition status through redaction, check the payout's paid-audit history manually when reconciling payouts.
- **Deferred Clover settlement follow-up residuals (Clover is not live yet):** W1 flagged a separate Clover settlement bug to resolve in its own PR. Tied to that Clover follow-up PR, five Clover-specific edge cases are intentionally deferred here:
  1. `reserve_hold`/`reserve_release` adjustments explicitly linked to a non-custodial/Clover order are not separately modelled in `non_custodial_settlement_bridge`.
  2. Mixed-cart `platform`-bearer processing splits on non-custodial orders are not split in `non_custodial_settlement_bridge` (Clover orders are organizer-direct card-present sales without platform-borne Stripe processing splits).
  3. Any platform service fees (`FeeComponent` `kind: "platform"`) attached to organizer-direct Clover orders are excluded from `fee_revenue_gross` pending CPA confirmation of whether HåfaPass collects platform fees on organizer-direct Clover sales and W1's Clover settlement follow-up.
  4. **A later Stripe capture can rewrite a closed BPT payout row on a Clover-only event** (PR #247 review round 2, R2-2; `TaxReports`, `tax_reports.rb` around line 120). Example: January's BPT detail CSV first excludes a $10 payout on a Clover-only event. In February a Stripe payment on an expired order captures late, and regenerating January now shows the payout, before the buyer is even refunded. The BPT export decides payout eligibility from any current Stripe capture on the event. Suggested fix: use payout custody evidence, or count a payout only when a qualifying Stripe capture was recorded by the payout's recording date.
  5. **The narrower CPA bridge exclusion can drop a legacy Clover balance** (PR #247 review round 2, R2-3; `never_fulfilled_capture_order_ids` in `cpa_monthly_report.rb`). Example: a completed legacy Clover order with a null `completed_at` has a $12.00 settlement bridge. Adding a legacy `sim_` Stripe payment row removes the whole event from the CPA report, because the bridge exclusion accepts a capture the custodial query rejects. Suggested fix: share one live-capture predicate between the bridge and the custodial query, and identify never-fulfilled orders by durable late-capture evidence (the reconciliation-exception test `CpaMonthlyReport#unfulfilled_capture_sql` now uses for buyer refunds), not by `completed_at IS NULL`.
- **Never-fulfilled capture cases resolved in the PR #247 follow-up (review round 3):**
  1. **R3-1, capture and exception either side of Guam midnight.** Classification now uses the exception linked to the capture's own payment and the capture's recording time, so both months agree and `opening = prior closing` holds for organizer and buyer columns.
  2. **R3-2, organizer-borne processing after the automatic refund.** `Settlements::Calculator` no longer charges the organizer for processing on a never-fulfilled capture; the payout statement shows it as covered by HåfaPass, matching this export. Needs Leon/CPA confirmation (see the switch above). This changes the settlement source digest only for events that have such processing; no other event's digest changes.
  3. **R3-3, lost dispute on a never-fulfilled capture.** No longer a `chargeback` and no longer debited on the payout statement. It clears the buyer balance, capped so a later refund cannot clear it twice.
- **Remaining never-fulfilled residual:** a manual partial refund of a never-fulfilled capture leaves the unrefunded ticket share in the buyer columns here, while the payout statement still counts it as organizer proceeds. The automatic refund always returns the whole payment, so this needs an operator to refund part of it by hand.
- **Legacy BPT event CSV:** The earlier compact CSV (`Export monthly BPT detail CSV` / `bpt_events_export` from PR #223) remains available for quick cross-checking, while `Export monthly CPA CSV` (`cpa_monthly_export`) is the complete recording-time period-stable monthly reconciliation workpaper.
