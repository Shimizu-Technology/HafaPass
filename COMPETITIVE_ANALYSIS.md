# HafaPass Competitive Analysis

Status: current market summary
Last verified: July 20, 2026 (Pacific/Guam)
Authoritative requirements: [docs/TICKETING_PLATFORM_BLUEPRINT.md](docs/TICKETING_PLATFORM_BLUEPRINT.md)

## Market position

HafaPass should not position itself as a generic Ticketmaster clone or merely a cheaper GuamTime. Its defensible position is a Guam-first event operating system that combines self-service software with local promotion, hospitality relationships, and event-day support.

The immediate segment is general-admission nightlife, cultural, community, school, fundraiser, workshop, hospitality, festival, and brand events. Assigned seating follows only after the general-admission commerce and operations platform is proven.

## GuamTime

GuamTime is active and has current event inventory. Previous HafaPass documentation stating that its ticketing site was down is no longer valid.

### Current strengths

- Local awareness and organizer trust.
- Human-assisted event setup and support.
- Event staff and mobile scanners.
- Assigned seating.
- Merchandise/concession and donation capability.
- Organizer reporting.
- Marketing distribution.
- Post-event payouts.

### Opportunity for HafaPass

- Faster verified self-service publishing.
- Modern mobile buyer and organizer experiences.
- Clear all-in fees with absorb/pass/split controls.
- Accurate real-time ledger-based reporting.
- Offline admission designed for unreliable venue connectivity.
- Automated and reconcilable organizer settlement.
- Secure transfers and wallet passes.
- Promoter attribution, hospitality integrations, and Ambros distribution.
- Local service equal to the software quality.

Source: [GuamTime Services](https://www.guamtime.net/services)

## Ticketmaster

Ticketmaster is a reference for high-trust ticket fulfillment and venue operations, not the initial scope.

### Adopt

- Revocable trusted digital ticket credentials.
- Barcode replacement after transfer.
- Controlled ticket transfer.
- Wallet passes.
- Inventory and entry analytics.
- Clear seat and accessibility information when seating is introduced.
- Useful, transparent add-ons.

### Do not prioritize

- Open resale marketplace.
- Speculative tickets.
- Opaque premium/dynamic pricing.
- Global fan identity graph.
- Large-arena complexity before Guam general admission is dependable.

Sources: [Ticketmaster ticket sales](https://business.ticketmaster.com/solutions/ticket-sales/), [Ticketmaster SafeTix](https://business.ticketmaster.com/safetix-encrypted-digital-ticketing/)

## Required competitive parity

Before a general-admission organizer should choose HafaPass for a meaningful event, the platform needs:

- Verified organizer onboarding.
- Dependable payment, refund, and payout reconciliation.
- Expiring inventory holds and zero overselling.
- Guest and authenticated mobile checkout.
- Apple Pay/Google Pay where supported.
- Secure QR fulfillment and ticket recovery.
- Offline multi-device admission.
- Staff roles and emergency door list.
- Promo, comp, refund, cancellation, and reschedule operations.
- Accurate real-time reporting and settlement statements.
- Local event-day support.

## Differentiators after parity

- Publish in minutes after verification.
- Guam/ChST correctness everywhere.
- Transparent lower fees without surprise checkout totals.
- Secure transfer and wallet support.
- Waitlist offers that reserve inventory fairly.
- Promoter/referral commission attribution.
- Donations, add-ons, merchandise, waivers, and registration questions.
- Curated Tonight/This Weekend/village/venue discovery.
- Hotel, concierge, tourism, Ambros, and hospitality distribution.
- Japanese and CHamoru support where content can be maintained.

## Pricing principles

Do not lock the product to the prototype’s current `3% + $0.50` configuration until merchant-of-record, processing costs, Guam tax treatment, support costs, reserves, refunds, and chargeback liability are decided.

The final model must:

- Show all-in buyer price before payment.
- Define whether free events are truly free.
- Allow approved absorb, pass, or split policies.
- Separate provider processing cost from HafaPass revenue.
- Preserve refund and fee rules in the immutable order ledger.
- Produce organizer settlement statements that explain every cent.

## Strategic conclusion

HafaPass can surpass GuamTime for general-admission events without recreating all of Ticketmaster. The winning sequence is financial integrity, event-time and publishing correctness, recoverable checkout, organizer payouts, offline admission, local support, then transfers/wallets/waitlist/add-ons and marketplace growth.
